Schedule Scanner v1.0.0

Setup installs Schedule Scanner and its local Ollama runtime. You do not need Python or a separate Ollama installation.

After the application files are installed, the AI reader setup window will download qwen2.5vl:3b. This requires internet and several GB of free disk space. Download time depends on your connection.

If the download is interrupted, open Schedule Scanner to retry. Once setup is complete, screenshots are processed locally.

Models are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ScheduleScanner\models and retained when you upgrade or uninstall the application.
